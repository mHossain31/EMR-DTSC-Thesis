## Research questions

RQ1 measurement validity; RQ2 forecasting; RQ3 control; RQ4 value of prediction (Tier 2); RQ5 side effects (docs/SPECIFICATION.md 4.1).

## Confirmatory comparisons (Flushing; each at light, moderate, heavy demand)

SESOI 2% of the reference total NOx. Outcome: total NOx (g) from tripinfo, paired by seed, delta% = 100 (Y_cond - Y_ref) / Y_ref.

| ID | Comparison | Test | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| PC1 | legacy_ml vs static | TOST, two one-sided paired Wilcoxon tests, margin +-2% | equivalence (near zero under the matched protocol) |
| PC2 | eco_ml vs static | two-sided paired Wilcoxon | difference in total NOx |
| PC3 | eco_ml vs tt_adaptive | two-sided paired Wilcoxon | difference in total NOx |
| PC4 | eco_ml vs eco_reactive | TOST, margin +-2% | equivalence (does forecasting add value?) |

Multiplicity: one Holm family over the 12 tests, familywise alpha 0.05. Equivalence is declared only if the Holm-adjusted TOST p-value is below 0.05. Reported for every test: mean and median delta%, 95% BCa CI (10,000 resamples, seed 20261115), unadjusted and Holm-adjusted p-values, d_z; for TOST also the 90% CI.

## Conditions and parameters

| Condition | Parameters |
|---|---|
| C0 static | free-flow routes from the route file |
| C1 tt_adaptive | rerouting device, probability 1, period 60 s, adaptation interval 10 s, 18 steps |
| C2 random_reroute | eta = 1 (route-change rate matched to C4 on validation seeds) |
| C3 eco_reactive | alpha* = 0.75; EMA 0.5; shrinkage vs/(vs+30) |
| C4 eco_ml | alpha* = 0.75; model gbm_v1 (SHA-256 aff0192553ccbac45f71fb144f5f29fd26c6ba5cc8f2b6a60623180bdb30cb84) |
| C5 legacy_ml | legacy RF, threshold at the legacy flag-share quantile of training NOx |

Alpha* rule: minimise mean total NOx subject to mean travel time <= +5% over tt_adaptive, on validation seeds, chosen separately for C3 and C4. Reroute period 60 s per vehicle (staggered), compliance 100%. C2-C4 share the travel-time term: edge length over the time-mean edge speed of the last 180 s, floored at free-flow time, and route with SUMO's travel-time router on the controller's edge weights (`edge.adaptTraveltime`, `vehicle.rerouteTraveltime`; DEVIATIONS #12-13).

## Protocol and validity

Step 1 s; departures 0-3600 s; run until every vehicle arrives (cap 14400 s); time-to-teleport 300 s; ignore-junction-blocker 30 s; collisions warn. Valid run: completion >= 99.5%, teleports <= 0.5% of trips, no crash or timeout. Confirmatory tests use pairs where both runs are valid; a sensitivity analysis uses all pairs. Invalid-run counts are reported per condition.

## Metrics

Primary: total NOx (g) = sum of tripinfo NOx_abs (mg) / 1000. Secondary: total CO2 (kg), NOx per vehicle-km, mean and 95th-percentile travel time, time loss, waiting time, vehicle-km, departure delay, reroutes and route-change rate, controller time per tick, teleports, collisions, completion. Spatial (RQ5): NOx share by road class, p99 5-minute edge NOx density, Gini of per-edge NOx.

## Sample size

Pilot SD of paired NOx changes 4.906% (largest over comparisons and levels); alpha per test 0.00417; 80% power needs n = 91 (superiority at 2%) and 101 (TOST at +-2%, true effect 0); confirmatory n = 60 (n = max(30, ceil(1.2 n_power)), capped at 60).

At n = 60 the simulated power is 52% for a true change of 2% (superiority) and 27% for showing equivalence within +-2% when the true change is 0 (TOST). 80% power is reached for a true change of 2.5% and for an equivalence margin of +-2.6%. The confirmatory tests are therefore underpowered for effects near the SESOI: a non-significant superiority test does not show that a 2% effect is absent, and equivalence that is not shown does not show a difference.
