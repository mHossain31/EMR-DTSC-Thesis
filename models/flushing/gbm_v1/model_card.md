# Model card: gbm_v1 (flushing)

Generated by `python -m nycemis train`; do not edit by hand.

- Task: forecast, for every edge, the next 60 s window's NOx per vehicle-second (r, mg/veh-s) and mean speed (v, m/s), from F1 traffic-state features only (no emission information); a third model forecasts NOx density (d, mg per lane-metre) for the hotspot evaluation.
- Model: lightgbm gradient-boosted trees; r and v on their own scale (squared error, so conditional means), d on the log1p scale; best parameters per target from 30 random-search trials with early stopping on validation seeds.
- Data: dataset experiment(s) dataset; train seeds 0-23, validation seeds 100-111; 2,755,475 training and 1,374,358 validation edge-windows (uniform 10% per run). Test seeds were not read.
- Hotspot threshold: 3.5301 mg per lane-metre per window (95th percentile of non-empty training edge-windows).
- Evaluated on: validation seeds.
